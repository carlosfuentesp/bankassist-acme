# Banco ACME
## Política de Cobranza de Créditos de Consumo

Policy ID: POL-COL-2026-01
Version: 2026
Effective from: 2026-01-01
Classification: COLLECTIONS
Status: ACTIVE

### 1. Purpose

This policy defines the collection actions available to Banco ACME analysts for consumer loans.

### 2. Customers between 31 and 60 days past due

A customer with a consumer loan between 31 and 60 days past due must be reviewed using:

- outstanding balance
- payment history
- payment promises
- previous payment arrangements

### 3. Active payment promise

If the customer has an ACTIVE payment promise and the promised payment date has not yet passed, the analyst should continue monitoring the existing promise.

A new payment arrangement should not be initiated while the active promise remains valid.

### 4. Broken payment promise

A customer with a BROKEN payment promise may be evaluated for a payment arrangement when all of the following conditions are met:

- the product is a consumer loan
- days past due are between 31 and 60
- outstanding balance is less than or equal to USD 2,500
- the customer has not had a payment arrangement during the previous 90 days

### 5. Decision responsibility

BankAssist may identify customers who satisfy the policy conditions.

BankAssist does not approve payment arrangements.

Final evaluation and approval remain the responsibility of an authorized Banco ACME collections analyst.
