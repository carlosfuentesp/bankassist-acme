# Banco ACME
## Política de Cobranza de Créditos de Consumo

Policy ID: POL-COL-2025-01
Version: 2025
Effective from: 2025-01-01
Effective to: 2025-12-31
Classification: INTERNAL
Status: SUPERSEDED

### 1. Purpose

This policy defines collection actions for Banco ACME consumer loans.

### 2. Customers between 31 and 60 days past due

Customers with consumer loans between 31 and 60 days past due may be evaluated for a payment arrangement.

The analyst must review:

- outstanding balance
- payment history
- previous payment promises
- previous payment arrangements

### 3. Payment arrangements

A customer may be evaluated for a payment arrangement when:

- days past due are between 31 and 60
- outstanding balance is below USD 3,000
- the customer has not had a payment arrangement during the previous 60 days

Final approval remains the responsibility of the collections analyst.
